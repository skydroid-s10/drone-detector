# Update procedure notes

This document is a research checklist, not a substitute for the manufacturer's instructions for a specific device.

Before an update:
1. Confirm the exact model and hardware revision.
2. Obtain firmware from a legitimate source and preserve the untouched original file.
3. Record filename, file size, source, acquisition date, and SHA-256.
4. Ensure stable power and follow the instructions applicable to that exact device.
5. Do not disconnect power or cables during an active update.
6. Do not use reconstructed or modified firmware unless compatibility, integrity, package requirements, and recovery options have been independently established.

For protocol research, record the update tool version, device-identification handshake, packet framing, acknowledgements/retries, integrity checks, finalisation, and reboot behaviour. Do not assume a tool is universal across S10-family devices without evidence.
